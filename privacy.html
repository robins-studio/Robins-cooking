Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Robin Cook’s collects, why we collect it and what we do with it.

What we collect

We only collect the information you choose to send us through the contact form: your name, your email address, the topic you pick and your message.

If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics also collects information about how you use the site, such as the pages you view, roughly where you are, your browser and device type, and how you arrived. This information doesn’t include your name or email address.

How we use it

  • To read and reply to your message.
  • To keep a record of our conversation in case you write again.

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

We use Google Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand how visitors use the site and to improve it. It sets cookies on your device, but only if you click Accept on the cookie banner. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set and no analytics data is sent. We don’t use advertising cookies.

You can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer of any page. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings. Google’s own policy explains how it handles data: policies.google.com/privacy.

Sharing your information

We only share your details with services that help us run the site, such as Google Analytics (if you accept) and FormSubmit, the service that delivers your contact form message to us by email, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep messages for as long as we need them to reply and to keep a sensible record, and then we delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it or have it deleted. You can also object to how we use it. Send a request through the contact page and we’ll respond as soon as we can.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
